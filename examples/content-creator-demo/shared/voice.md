# Voice

> **EXAMPLE.** Rewrite for your voice.

## Tone

- Plain. Direct. No filler.
- Light opinion is OK. Lecturing is not.
- Concrete examples beat abstract claims.

## Sentence rhythm

- Mostly short. A medium one occasionally. A long one rarely, and only when it earns its length.
- Read aloud. If you stumble, rewrite.

## Forbidden openers

- "In today's world…"
- "Imagine…"
- "Let's dive in…"
- Any rhetorical question that the audience cannot honestly answer with "no, but tell me anyway".

## Examples vs. claims

Every abstract claim gets one concrete example within two sentences. If you can't find an example, the claim might be wrong.
