# Research Done Checklist

Use this to evaluate whether the research output is ready to hand to the script stage.

- [ ] One-sentence restatement of the topic.
- [ ] At least 5 key points, each phrased as a claim (not a question).
- [ ] Each claim has at least one concrete example.
- [ ] At least 2 sources cited with URL.
- [ ] At least 2 narrative angles (different ways to enter the topic).
- [ ] At least 1 open question marked clearly as unresolved.
- [ ] No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive").
