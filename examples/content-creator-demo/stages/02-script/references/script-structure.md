# Script Structure Template

Use this as the spine for any script.

## 1. Hook (0:00–0:15)

One concrete image, story, or number. No abstraction. No throat-clearing.

## 2. Stakes (0:15–0:30)

Why should the audience care? Concrete consequence of getting this right vs wrong.

## 3. The Idea (0:30–1:30)

Plain explanation of the core idea. One example per claim.

## 4. The Twist or Depth (1:30–3:00)

Something the audience would not have guessed. A counterintuitive consequence, a historical parallel, a real case where it played out.

## 5. The Practical (3:00–4:30)

What does the audience actually do with this? Two or three concrete actions.

## 6. The Ending (4:30–5:00)

One sentence that lands. No "thanks for watching". No CTA dump. Earn the next click instead.
