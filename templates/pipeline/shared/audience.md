# Audience

> **EXAMPLE.** Rewrite for your audience.

## Who

Technical-curious non-engineers. They use software daily. They've heard of APIs. They have not written one.

## What they know

- Basic computer literacy. Files, folders, browser tabs.
- They've used ChatGPT or Claude at least once.
- They follow tech news at a headline level.

## What they don't know

- They don't read code.
- They don't have intuition for "this would be easy" vs "this would be hard" in software.
- They are skeptical of hype but not cynical.

## What earns their trust

- A worked example they can replicate in their own life.
- An honest "I don't know" when you don't.
- Specific numbers (15 minutes, $1.20, 30,000 people) over vague ones ("many", "fast", "cheap").
