# Content Creator Workspace — Overview

> **EXAMPLE.**

## What this workspace does

Houses everything for a single-person content operation. Three modes of work: think/write, build, distribute. Each lives in its own subfolder.

## Workspaces

| Folder | Mode | Inputs | Outputs |
|--------|------|--------|---------|
| `script-lab/` | think, draft | ideas, research | finished scripts |
| `production/` | build | scripts, brand | edited assets, exports |
| `distribution/` | publish | finished assets | scheduled posts, analytics |

## When in doubt

- Where do I write? → The workspace you're in. Not the others.
- Where's the voice guide? → `shared/voice.md`.
- Where's the brand? → `_config/brand.md`.
